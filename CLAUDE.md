# Project

Automated kids' YouTube video pipeline. `make_video.py` builds one video; GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/make-video.yml`) runs it on a schedule and publishes each video as a GitHub Release for the owner to review and upload by hand.

# Goal

Maximize profit from the channel. Act like a co-owner: without being asked, judge every output against what top kids' channels with millions of views do (retention, pacing, color, characters, titles, thumbnails), name the weaknesses honestly, and fix or propose the highest-impact improvement next. Look for growth and revenue levers (formats, compilations, Shorts, upload timing, playlists, extra income) and bring them up.
